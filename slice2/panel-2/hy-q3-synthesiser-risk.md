1. **Consistency lie — the state is served as “now” when its last correction is old, or its tolerance is narrower than the truth (includes shed-blind extrapolation).**  
   *Mechanism:* The synthesiser keeps extrapolating through a correction gap. The gap is caused by source death, ingest backpressure/shedding (§3.9: cameras “drop frames, newest wins”; feeds capped by LensFile), or perception yield (§2.3 L3 reflex-only). The state file still carries a plausible `tolerance_band`, but `extrapolation_age_ms` has outrun the stream’s measured rhythm. The projector (shelf tile, player, rule engine, or console) renders it without the `extrapolated/stale` badge, so a late reader cannot tell a corrected fact from a guess. The worst variant is *shed-blindness*: ingest shed 100% of frames during a GPU-saturation window, `events.jsonl` receipted the drops, but the synthesier’s error model never saw a correction, so tolerance was never widened and the feed stayed silent.  
   *Household manifestation:* “Is the back door open?” → “Closed, as of now” when the last good frame was 90 s ago and the door opened 10 s after it. A door-camera rule pauses the film for a person who was never there, or — worse — a dead camera loops a quiet frame and the feed says “dock: quiet” with no `stream_down` entry. The house gets confident silence, the exact failure Spec C §4.3 calls the product’s worst mode.  
   *Ledger evidence that must exist:* `streams/<id>/state.txt` must carry `as_of_stamp`, `last_correction_stamp`, `source_tick`, `extrapolation_age_ms`, `tolerance_band`, `staleness_class` (`fresh|extrapolating|stale|dead`), and `shed_fraction` for the trailing window. `events.jsonl` must log every correction with `arrival_stamp`, `source_stamp`, `correction_latency_ms`, and `innovation` (predicted-vs-observed residual), plus every shed with `reason` and `count`. Cross-checks: (a) if `extrapolation_age_ms > lens.period_ema + 3*sqrt(lens.jitter_ema_variance)`, force `stale`, never `now`; (b) `shed_fraction > 0` in the window ⇒ label the state `degraded` and suspend the stream’s `trust_accounting` precision estimate; (c) `health.txt` must raise `consecutive_missed_intervals` and emit one `stream_down` feed entry (§2.8), and `attention/feed.jsonl` entries must carry `basis: corrected|extrapolated|stale` and `tolerance`. A `sla_violations` counter increments whenever `extrapolation_age_ms` exceeds `lens.max_extrapolation_ms`.

2. **Tolerance mislearning — the error model is fit on a biased regime and then meets the real world.**  
   *Mechanism:* The synthesier learns `drift_model` / `tolerance_band` from EMA over corrections (α=0.10, §3.1) and from `baseline_embeddings` admitted only when the sample looked “normal.” If the learning window is a quiet regime — dock at 03:00, nursery while the baby sleeps, a feed in its off-season, or a week of abnormal weather — the EMA sits near zero and the band is too tight; the first real change is then either absorbed as “within tolerance” (missed event) or, if the band was inflated by a noisy regime (sprinklers, rain, a storm week), a real change is clamped as noise. The lens is also contaminated if a standing intruder becomes furniture (§3.2 baseline-admission rule) or if `ranker_calibration` was refit on <48 h of data and is still `lens: warming`.  
   *Household manifestation:* The nursery monitor is set to “sip,” learns “no movement = normal,” and then does not publish when the child actually wakes; or the harbour feed, calibrated on a calm week, floods the change feed with “boat moved” on the first windy day. The feed either goes mute or cries wolf; precision drops below the 0.9 demotion bar (§2.2) and the stream is demoted to feed-only, but only after the household has already lost trust.  
   *Ledger evidence:* `lens.txt` must expose `drift_model.ema_alpha`, `drift_model.window_start`, `drift_model.sample_count`, `regime_tag` (e.g. `learned_during: night|quiet|storm`), `baseline_embeddings.spread`, `ranker_calibration.last_refit`, `ranker_calibration.audit_sample_count`, and the 24 hourly `drift_quantiles`. `events.jsonl` must keep the `(reflex_score, true_drift)` audit pairs. Cross-checks: (a) a `tolerance_exceedance_rate` counter = fraction of trailing 100 corrections whose `innovation` exceeds `tolerance_band`; if >5% for two consecutive refits, force a recalibration event and widen the band by a fixed factor (e.g. 2×) rather than trusting the learned value; (b) if `lens_age < 48h` or `audit_sample_count < N`, the state must be labelled `lens: warming` and tolerance must not be the learned EMA; (c) `pending_drift_accumulator` and `slow-change` publishes (§3.2) must be visible, so slow-but-real movement is not hidden inside a too-tight band; (d) `trust_accounting` (fired/confirmed/dismissed, 7-day precision) must be written per stream and is the demotion trigger.

3. **State poisoning — a bad, frozen, or lying sensor corrects the world state to a false value.**  
   *Mechanism:* A thin device (Amendment 1/2: phone-as-camera, any browser terminal), an RTSP camera that freezes on a last frame, a looping test pattern, or a misconfigured score/weather feed sends samples that pass ingest’s dumb adapter. Because ingest only stamps arrival and hashes files (§3.1), a continuous stream can deliver a repeated `content_ref` or an out-of-range value. The synthesier accepts it as a correction: the `innovation` is small relative to a (possibly mislearned) tolerance, so the state is *actively* wrong rather than merely stale. A malicious or confused device on the LAN can also claim a stream id and inject items with valid arrival stamps.  
   *Household manifestation:* The console shows a “live” camera tile that is a still frame from ten minutes ago; a door-camera verdict says “person” and Rule 1 (§2.3) pauses the living-room film for a ghost; a weather feed says 72°F when it is 32°F, so the household dresses wrong; a stuck audio embed repeats “glass break” and the change feed fills with false urgency. Unlike #1, the state is not old — it is confidently incorrect.  
   *Ledger evidence:* `items/<item-id>.txt` must carry `content_ref` (hash), `source_device_id`, `device_fingerprint`, `adapter_version`, `arrival_stamp`, and `source_stamp`; `streams/<id>/state.txt` must carry `source_device_id`, `device_trust_state` (`provisional|trusted|quarantined`), and `witness_set` (which streams/devices corroborate the current verdict). `events.jsonl` must log each correction’s `innovation`, `residual`, `sensor_id`, and `device_health`. Cross-checks: (a) `duplicate_content_hash_run` and `frames_since_change` in `health.txt`: if the incoming hash repeats for >N consecutive items (e.g. 30) while `lens.period_ema` predicts change, raise `frozen_source` and stop treating the stream as live; (b) a per-class `max_physical_delta` in `lens.txt` (temperature cannot jump 40°F in one poll, a score cannot move 50 points) — any correction whose `innovation` exceeds it is rejected, not applied, and logged as `poisoned_correction_rejected` with the sample retained; (c) new thin devices start `provisional`: their corrections enter a quarantine extrapolation with a widened tolerance until 10 corroborated samples or a user confirmation; (d) any rule that actuates across streams (door→pause) must require `corroboration_count >= 2` or a `jev_p` in the recorded advisor band, and a `single_witness` flag must be set when only one source backs the verdict.
